Vandit Patel

PhD AI @ Representation Learning Lab, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.

I am a PhD student in the Representation Learning Lab at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, advised by Prof Prathosh AP. I am broadly interested in understanding how learning systems represent information, how those representations shape their behaviour, and whether we can interact with models at a level deeper than their input-output behaviour.

Before starting my PhD, I spent two years working in AI research at Stryker, where I worked on large language models, generative modelling, post-training, and agentic AI systems. That experience made me increasingly interested in questions that are harder to answer by engineering alone: why models behave the way they do, what exactly they learn internally, and how reliably we can control or modify them.

I completed my M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence at IISc, where I worked in the Machine Learning Lab with Prof Chiranjib Bhattacharyya. My master’s research focused on sequential decision-making and optimal stopping. Before that, I studied Electrical Engineering at Nirma University.

My current interests span representation learning, generative modelling, language models, mechanistic understanding of neural networks, and methods for directly working with learned representations and model parameters. I particularly enjoy problems that sit somewhere between theory and experimentation: questions where a simple experiment can expose something fundamental about how a model works.

Outside individual projects, what interests me most is understanding intelligent systems as objects rather than only using them as tools. I like mathematics, building experiments, reading across seemingly unrelated areas, and following questions far enough to find out whether an intuitive idea actually survives contact with evidence.

I am always happy to talk about research, exchange ideas, or collaborate on interesting problems.
